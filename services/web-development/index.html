THE DETAILS

What kind of website do you need?

A focused business website should explain your offer clearly, work well on phones, and make it easy for visitors to contact you. A web application needs more: accounts, workflows, integrations, and a reliable backend. We scope these differently so the build fits its purpose.

We can create landing pages, company websites, online stores, custom dashboards, and web platforms. Design, content structure, accessibility, performance, and search visibility are considered from the start.

How we build and launch

We begin with your audience, goals, pages, and required features. The team then plans the information architecture and interface, develops the site, and tests it across devices and browsers before launch.

For content-heavy sites, we can provide an admin panel or CMS so your team can update pages. We also plan analytics, redirects from old URLs, backups, and ongoing maintenance where needed.

Website pricing and scope

The advertised starting price of ₹2,999 applies to an entry-level website scope. A tailored quote depends on page count, copy and design needs, content management, integrations, e-commerce, and support. We confirm the deliverables before work begins.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Can CloudEmpire build a website for my business in Kolkata?

Yes. CloudEmpire is based in Kolkata and works with businesses on responsive websites and web applications. We can discuss the pages, features, and launch plan remotely or locally.

Can I edit my website after it launches?

Yes, if content editing is part of the project scope. We can build an admin panel or connect a suitable CMS for pages, articles, images, and other agreed content.

Does the ₹2,999 offer include every website feature?

No. It is a starting price for a limited website scope. Custom design, extra pages, e-commerce, integrations, and ongoing services are quoted according to requirements.
