Launch an Android product that is understandable to users and maintainable for your team.

Plan the Android app around its users

We begin with the tasks people need to complete, the range of devices they use, and the data the app needs. That informs navigation, offline behavior, permissions, and accessibility before development begins.

If an existing backend or website is involved, we define the API and account model early so the app and supporting systems stay in sync.

Build, test, and release

Development follows agreed milestones with working builds to review. Testing covers core journeys, screen sizes, network conditions, and the permissions the app requests.

For release, we prepare the agreed store assets and support submission to Google Play. After launch, crash reports and user feedback guide updates.

Frequently asked questions

Can you build an Android app from an existing website?

Yes, but the right approach depends on the website and the features users need on mobile. We assess whether a responsive web app, cross-platform app, or dedicated Android app provides the better experience.

Do you help with Google Play publishing?

Yes. We can help prepare builds and store materials and support submission through your Google Play developer account as part of the agreed scope.
