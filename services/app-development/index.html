Choose the right mobile approach

Native Android and iOS apps can make sense when platform-specific features or device performance are central to the product. A cross-platform app can be effective when the same core experience should reach both ecosystems. We assess the trade-offs against your users, budget, and release plan.

The app is only one part of a useful product. We also plan authentication, APIs, data storage, analytics, notifications, and the support needed after launch.

From prototype to store release

We map essential tasks, prototype key screens, build the agreed features, and test on representative devices. Before release, we review privacy needs, store assets, permissions, and performance.

Once the app is live, updates and feedback help prioritize the next improvements.

Frequently asked questions

Should I build Android and iOS apps separately?

It depends on the product. Native development offers direct access to each platform, while cross-platform development can share more code. We recommend an approach after reviewing features, devices, timeline, and maintenance needs.

Can CloudEmpire help publish an app?

Yes. App Store and Play Store release support can be included in the project scope, along with testing, store materials, and guidance on account requirements.
